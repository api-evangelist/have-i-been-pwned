HVAC wholesale distributor Baker Distributing was compromised by the ShinyHunters group in May 2026, with attackers accessing SharePoint and Salesforce systems and exposing roughly 103,000 email addresses alongside names, physical addresses, phone numbers, and support tickets from contractor customers. The firm characterized the leaked material as largely corporate contact and support information with limited sensitivity.
